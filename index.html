Charter Uploader

A personal tool for scheduling my own music videos into my own TikTok drafts.

Charter Uploader is a small script I wrote for myself. I make music, I record short vertical videos of it, and this tool sends them to my own TikTok account as drafts on a schedule, so I do not have to upload them by hand one at a time. I then review each draft in the TikTok app and publish it myself.

How it works

  • I put my finished videos into a folder on my computer.
  • The script signs in to my own TikTok account through TikTok Login.
  • Twice a day it sends the next video to my TikTok inbox as a draft, using the official Content Posting API.
  • I open the TikTok app, check the draft, write the caption and publish it.

What it does not do

  • It has no users other than me, no sign-up and no accounts.
  • It never touches anyone else's data or content.
  • It does not automate likes, follows, comments or views.
  • It does not try to bypass any TikTok limit, restriction or moderation mechanism.
